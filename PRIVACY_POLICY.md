# Privacy Policy

Spectra is built as a fully open-source, privacy-first application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

## Information Collection and Use

Spectra does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data. All data (playback history, credentials, cache, preferences) is stored strictly on your local device.

Spectra does not include telemetry, tracking scripts, or analytics.

## Log Data

Log files generated during playback or debugging are saved locally on your device for troubleshooting purposes only and are never transmitted to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

When accessing third-party metadata or audio streams (such as YouTube, Piped, Invidious, or Last.fm), those services operate under their respective privacy policies. Spectra does not share your identity with these services.

## Contact

If you have questions or suggestions about Spectra's Privacy Policy, contact us via GitHub: https://github.com/rodrigo47363/spectra/issues
